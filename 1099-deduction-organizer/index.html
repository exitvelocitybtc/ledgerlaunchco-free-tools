Copy the checklist

- Income log: I have one place for every 1099, cash, app, and platform payment. - Expense categories: I can separate software, supplies, fees, mileage, home office notes, and other business expenses. - Receipt habit: I know where the receipt or proof lives for each business expense. - Mileage log: I track date, purpose, start/end, and business miles close to when the drive happens. - Quarterly set-aside: I have a recurring monthly or quarterly place to estimate what I may need to set aside. - Year-end summary: I can hand one organized summary to my tax pro instead of rebuilding the year from bank history. - Business only: I avoid mixing personal purchases with business expense records. - Review rhythm: I review the tracker weekly or monthly, not once at tax time.
