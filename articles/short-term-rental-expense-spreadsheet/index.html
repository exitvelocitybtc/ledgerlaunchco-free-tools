Short-Term Rental Expense Spreadsheet: Track Cleaning, Turnover, and Net by Stay

For Airbnb and VRBO hosts: expenses happen at the stay level but are reviewed too late at the month level.

Quick answer: Start by tracking the money event that creates confusion, then connect it to the actual payout, unpaid balance, or job profit. This page is educational organization only, not tax, legal, financial, or professional advice.

Stay-level expenses are easier to manage

A short-term rental expense spreadsheet works best when costs attach to the stay that caused them. Cleaning, restocking, repairs, refunds, and guest-specific adjustments are easier to understand while the booking is still fresh.

If every expense waits until the end of the month, the host has to reconstruct what happened from bank records and platform screens.

Stay-level tracking does not need to be complicated. It just needs a clear place for each cost.

Cleaning and turnover deserve their own view

Cleaning fee charged and cleaner cost should not be blended together. A host needs to know whether the fee covers the work, supplies, laundry, and restock patterns.

Track cleaning fee charged, cleaner cost, restock cost, laundry or linen cost, and turnover notes. That gives each stay a clearer net picture.

Over time, this can reveal whether a pricing or process change is needed.

Property-level comparison

If you host more than one unit, a single total is not enough. Each property should show gross, payout, expenses, net, nights, occupancy, and ADR.

The best property may not be the one with the highest gross revenue. It may be the one with stronger net after cleaning, platform fees, and turnover.

FAQ

What is the difference between STR expense tracking and a booking log?

A booking log tracks stay details. Expense tracking connects those stays to fees, cleaning, turnover costs, payouts, and net results.

Should cleaning fees be tracked as income?

Track cleaning fee charged and cleaner cost separately so the margin is visible. How you report anything professionally is outside this educational organizer.

Can this work for VRBO too?

Yes. A platform column lets the same structure work for Airbnb, VRBO, direct bookings, and other channels.
